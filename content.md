### 3.6 Join Us (Careers)

**Working at Avira**

Avira is a firm where the quality of the work and the culture around it matter equally.

The work is varied and often complex, and people are expected to engage with it beyond the immediate task at hand — understanding the wider context of a case, thinking about strategy, and contributing to how the work develops. It makes for a practice that is intellectually interesting and gives people a real stake in the matters they work on.

That approach carries through to the way the firm works. Knowledge is shared generously, discussions are candid, and different perspectives are welcomed. People are trusted to take responsibility for their work while drawing on the experience and insight of those around them.

Avira offers the opportunity to do engaging work in a firm that combines high standards with an open, collaborative and genuinely collegial culture.

**Join Avira**

We welcome applications from lawyers at all levels of experience. If you would like to be considered, please write to **careers@aviralaw.com** with your CV. A short note about the work that interests you is welcome.

**Internships**

We welcome internship applications from law students throughout the year. To apply, send your CV, year of study and preferred internship dates to **careers@aviralaw.com**.
